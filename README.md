# Are Fossil Fuel Subsidies Declining? Two Persuasive Visualizations

**Live report: [ishaantibdewal.github.io/dsc106_project2](https://ishaantibdewal.github.io/dsc106_project2/)**

DSC 106 (Data Visualization) Project 2 at UC San Diego. Using the IMF Fossil Fuel Subsidies dataset, I
built two pages that argue opposite sides of one claim, **"Global fossil fuel subsidies are declining"**,
from the same data:

1. **Yes, they are declining:** truncated axes, a start year at a local peak, five hand-picked countries,
   and upbeat titles.
2. **No, they are not:** zero-based axes, the full 2015–2030 series including projections, and every top
   emitter.

The [write-up](WRITEUP.md) scores each of the ten design decisions from honest to deceptive, explains
why the "not declining" side is the honest one (subsidies rose 58% since 2015), and summarizes the techniques that make a chart persuasive
or misleading.
